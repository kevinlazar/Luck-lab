Projections

Each player's usage-based points per game (70% expected, 30% actual, with last season as a small prior), adjusted for every game left: the team's Vegas total, the opponent's run defense on the kind of runs he makes (inside vs outside gaps), its pass defense, deep defense, points allowed to his position, and for receivers how he has done against man vs zone coverage. Scored with each league's own settings.

Trade value

Rest-of-season points above a replacement starter in your league, with playoff weeks counted 1.5 times. Dynasty adds the next three seasons, adjusted for age and position, and values draft picks against players. Both are anchored to the trade market: crowd-sourced values from thousands of real fantasy trades (FantasyCalc) plus where the player went in your league's own draft, which fades as the season goes on. "How they see it" leans mostly on that market, with points scored so far on top.

Run style

Inside runs are runs up the middle or through the guard gaps; outside runs go through the tackle or end. Free data doesn't label zone or gap blocking schemes, so direction is the stand-in. Coverage tendencies come from last season's charting.

Expected points

From nflverse's open expected-points model. It grades every carry, target and pass attempt by field position, down, distance and air yards, then adds up what an average player scores on those plays.

Scoring

The luck board uses full PPR: 1 per catch, 0.1 per rushing or receiving yard, 0.04 per passing yard, 6 per rushing or receiving TD, 4 per passing TD, −2 per interception or lost fumble. Projections and trade values use each league's own scoring.

Usage

Snap % is the share of team offensive plays he was on the field for. Target share is his targets divided by team targets. RZ touches are carries and catches inside the 20. GL carries are carries inside the 5.

Refresh

Player data comes from nflverse and rebuilds automatically every morning, plus a Sunday pre-kickoff update. Your Sleeper leagues load live every time you open the page. Small samples swing wildly early in the season, so trust the numbers more as the weeks pile up.
